Questions

Before you create 💗

Is WishingStar free?

Yes. The current creator flow is free and does not ask for payment or contact details.

Do I have to add photos or music?

No. Photos/videos and music are optional. You can make a beautiful surprise with just the required personal details and letter.

How many memories can I add?

You can add up to 8 photos/videos in total. Photos are limited to 5 MB each and videos to 15 MB each.

Can I add my own music file?

Yes. Custom audio is optional, limited to 5 MB, and one file can be used at a time.

Can I make something other than a birthday surprise?

Yes. Choose Proposal, Valentine, Anniversary or Custom, and add optional sections such as Why I Love You, Our Little Promises or Date Night.

Do they need an app?

No. The surprise opens in a normal mobile or desktop browser.
